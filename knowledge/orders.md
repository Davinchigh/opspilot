# Orders and payment

## Lead time
Celebration cakes need at least 3 days notice. Two tier cakes need 7 days notice. Pastry boxes need 1 day notice.

## Payment
We accept mobile money, bank transfer and cards. A 50 percent deposit confirms a cake order. The balance is due on delivery or pickup.

## Cancellation
Orders cancelled at least 48 hours before the delivery date get a full refund of the deposit. Later cancellations lose the deposit.
