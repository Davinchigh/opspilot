# Policies

## Complaints and refunds
If something is wrong with an order, send a photo within 24 hours. A manager reviews every complaint and decides on a replacement or refund.

## Allergies
Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, milk, peanuts and tree nuts. We cannot promise that any product is free from allergens.

## Opening hours
The shop is open from 8 am to 7 pm Monday to Saturday, and closed on Sunday.
