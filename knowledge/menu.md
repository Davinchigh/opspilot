# Menu and prices
Sample data for the fictional Sweet Crumb Bakery.

## Cakes
A 6 inch celebration cake costs GHS 180. An 8 inch celebration cake costs GHS 260. A two tier cake starts at GHS 550.
Flavours are vanilla, chocolate, red velvet and carrot.

## Pastries
A box of 6 cupcakes costs GHS 60. A box of 12 doughnuts costs GHS 110. A sausage roll costs GHS 12. A loaf of banana bread costs GHS 45. A cinnamon roll costs GHS 18.
